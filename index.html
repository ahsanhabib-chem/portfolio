Analytical & Environmental Chemistry · Molecular Spectroscopy

Md. Ahsan Habib

M.S. Researcher, Department of Chemistry, University of Rajshahi

I study what soil organic carbon is made of. My M.S. thesis uses UV–Visible and FTIR spectroscopy, with Python data analysis, to describe the quality of organic carbon in the kaolinite-rich soils of Bangladesh's Varendra (Barind) Tract. I also characterize compost, and I co-authored a review on microplastics and nanoplastics in the atmosphere. I lead a four-member research team under Dr. Md. Nurul Islam.

Featured Projects

From soil spectroscopy to compost chemistry.

M.S. Thesis · Lead researcher

Soil Organic Carbon Quality in Varendra Soils

A study of 20 soil samples from three Upazilas of Chapainawabganj District, using two complementary spectroscopic methods.

  • UV–Vis indices (E2/E3, E4/E6, spectral slopes and slope ratio) from water extracts.
  • FTIR bands of whole soil assigned to functional groups and kaolinite and quartz.
  • Python scripts for peak picking, indices and figures.
